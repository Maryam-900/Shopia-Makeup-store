---

## 💎 Key Features

### 🌌 3D Identity Portal
A breathtaking **3D Login & Registration** gateway featuring:
- **Perspective Tilt**: The portal card reacts dynamically to mouse movement for a true 3D feel.
- **Membership Management**: Save and access your luxury identity via a secure local storage system.
- **Glassmorphic Design**: Deep blur effects and ethereal background animations for a premium first impression.

### 🤖 AI Identity Lab
The heart of Shophia's innovation. Choose from three distinct AI personas, each with its own standalone interactive showroom:
- **Signature Lipstick AI**: Sophisticated, professional, and detailed.
- **Beauty Bestie AI**: Playful, energetic, with heart-pop and sparkle animations.
- **Magic Transformation AI**: Artistic and editorial, featuring muted rose sparkles and deep plum aesthetics.

### 🛍️ The Essentials Collection
A fully functional high-end shopping experience:
- **Dynamic Inventory**: A curated grid of luxury products from Serums to Cinematic Eyeshadow palettes.
- **Universal Search**: An ultra-fast search overlay that makes finding your beauty match effortless.
- **State Management**: Real-time Cart and Wishlist management powered by browser storage.

## 🎨 Design System

Our aesthetic is built on the pillars of **Luxury, Clarity, and Motion**.

- **Palette**: 
  - `Shophia Plum (#4A0E2E)` - Depth & Sophistication
  - `Shophia Rose (#8E5B6E)` - Grace & Softness
  - `Shophia Gold (#D4AF37)` - Excellence & Value
  - `Shophia Silver (#C0C0C0)` - Modern Purity
- **Typography**: 
  - `Playfair Display` for headlines (Elegant, Serif)
  - `Inter` for interface elements (Clean, Productive)
- **Visual Effects**: Heavy use of **Backdrop Blur (Glassmorphism)**, **3D Transforms**, and **Micro-animations** (pulse, floating, particle pings).
